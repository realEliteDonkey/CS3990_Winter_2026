Weight Lifting

My favourite day-to-day hobby is weight lifting.
I find the enjoyment of physical progress, coupled with the euphoric feeling of

Archery

Perfect for spring until late fall. Target practice with the bow
is a fun way to spend time off, or end a chaotic day.
When you're on the range, no matter what else is going on in life,
shooting a bow forces you to quiet your mind. Archery has to be
one of the best natural stress relievers that I have experienced.
